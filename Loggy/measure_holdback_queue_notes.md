# Holdback Queue Measurement Notes

## Conclusions

- From our tests, the largest observed holdback queue contained 26 etries with Sleep = 100 and Jitter = 0. The tests showed that increasing Jitter from 0 to 3000 decreases the peak unsafe entries from 26 to 15. This is due to jutter blocking a worker between sending a message another worker and communicating with loggy. Therefore fewer events are produced and fewer entries remain in the queue waiting for the clock of the slowest worker to increase.

- Similarly, increasing Sleep from 100 to 2000 reduced the observed peak to 9 since sleep makes workers sent messages less frequently.

- The size is theoritically unbounded, since if one worker stops increasing its timestamp, its clock value in the logger gets stuck. This leads to the clocks minimum value getting stuck to a smaller value as other workers continue producing unsafe log entries forever.
